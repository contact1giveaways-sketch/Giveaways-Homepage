Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Giveaways Community ("we", "us") collects when you use giveawayscommunity.com and our instant prizes platform at giveawayscommunity.com/play, why we use it, who we share it with and what your rights are.

If you have any questions, email us at contact1.giveaways@gmail.com or message us on Telegram @suportGiveaways.

Who can use the platform

You must be 18 or over to create an account. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Email address and passwordTo create and secure your account. Passwords are stored encrypted by our login provider. We never see them.
Your name as shown on RaffallTo match you with the public entrants lists of our competitions, give you your spins and pay your prizes. It is shown publicly next to prizes you win.
Phone number (optional)To reach you quickly if you win, and to understand which countries our players come from.

Self-exclusion recordsTo keep your account (and any account with the same email or phone) blocked for the period you chose.

We also use the public entrants lists of our Raffall competitions (name and number of tickets) to give spins to ticket buyers. We don't receive any other data from Raffall.

Our legal reasons for using your data

  • To provide the service you signed up for: your account, spins, credits and prizes.
  • Your consent: emails, push notifications and your phone number. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the platform secure, preventing fraud and multiple accounts, and improving the service.
  • Legal obligations: keeping prize records and honouring self-exclusions.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use a few trusted providers to run the platform. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database and login (servers in London, UK).
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications.
  • Resend: sending emails.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK (for example in the USA). When they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as standard contractual clauses.

Your Raffall name and the prizes you win appear in public winner lists on the site.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account exists. You can delete it yourself from Wallet → Account, unless you have prizes on it (then we'll help you on Telegram).
  • Prize and draw records: kept as long as needed for transparency and our legal obligations, even after an account is deleted.
  • Self-exclusion records: kept while the exclusion is active and for as long as needed to honour it, even if the account is deleted.

Emails and notifications

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also turn emails and push notifications off at any time in Wallet → Account, or in your device settings.

Taking a break (self-exclusion)

You can block your account for 1, 2 or 3 months, or indefinitely, from Wallet → Account. During that time you can't spin or receive spins and we won't contact you. A break can't be cancelled before its end date. An indefinite break can only be lifted at your written request, after at least 6 months.

Free, confidential help: National Gambling Helpline 0808 8020 133 (GamCare), BeGambleAware, GAMSTOP.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a few small items so the site works: your login session, your notification choice and settings such as sound on or off.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data (unless we must keep it, see above);
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • send your data to you or another provider in a common format.

Email contact1.giveaways@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes we'll also let you know on the site.
